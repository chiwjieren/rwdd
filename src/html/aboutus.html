About GoGreenTogether

GoGreenTogether is a community platform to encourage eco-friendly habits and collective action. We provide resources, events, a green marketplace, and tips to help communities live more sustainably.

Our Goals

  • Educate communities on sustainable living
  • Make swapping and reuse easy via the marketplace
  • Host events and workshops to grow participation
